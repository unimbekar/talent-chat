Janus Soft Inc. Employee Benefits Summary
Plan year 2026–27

43300 Southern Walk Plaza, Suite 619, Ashburn, VA 20148
Phone: 703-599-9460 (office). Email: contact@janus-soft.com

Prepared for candidates considering Janus Soft. This is the 2026–27 benefits program that sits with an offer: coverage, time away, retirement, pay, and the allowances beside salary. Every figure is the term in the company summary.

100% — Employee health premium
Unlimited — Paid time off
10% — 401(k) match
$10,000 — Referral bonus

01  ·  Care
Medical, dental, and vision
The company pays the full premium for the employee and shares the cost for family members.
• Janus Soft pays 100% of the premium for the employee, and a generous contribution for other family members, for health, dental, and vision insurance.
• Coverage is effective the first of the next month from the date of hire.
Coverage | Employee contribution | Employer contribution | Effective date
Employee only | 0% | 100% | 1st of the month after the date of hire
Spouse and children | 50% | 50% | 1st of the month after the date of hire

02  ·  Time
Paid time off
Time away is part of the offer, not a balance you have to earn week by week.
• Unlimited paid time off, subject to meeting the minimum work-hours requirement.

03  ·  Future
Retirement
The 401(k) is a safe-harbor plan, and the match follows the employee’s own contribution.
• Janus Soft offers a 401(k) plan through safe harbor.
• The company matches 10% of the employee contribution.
• The company pays dollar for dollar up to 10% of the employee contribution.

04  ·  Pay
Compensation
Pay is meant to compete, and extra recognition does not wait for a review cycle.
• Highly competitive salary. Janus Soft Inc. pays its employees top dollars and pays one of the best salaries.
• Annual and spot bonus. Spot bonuses are based on feedback from the employee’s supervisor.
• Employee referral bonus.
• Employee performance incentives.

05  ·  People
Employee referral
Introducing someone who is hired is paid as a real bonus, not a token.
• Janus Soft pays a referral bonus of up to $10,000.00, spread over three months, for each successfully hired employee, for the life of that employment.

06  ·  Protection
Disability and life insurance
Income protection and life insurance are company-paid on the schedule below.
• Janus Soft offers short-term disability, long-term disability, and life insurance.
• The contribution schedule on file lists life insurance and short-term disability. Both are paid in full by the company.
Coverage | Employee contribution | Employer contribution | Effective date
Life insurance | 0% | 100% | 1st of the month, 60 days after the date of hire
Short-term disability | 0% | 100% | 1st of the month, 60 days after the date of hire

07  ·  Growth
Training and tuition
Skill-building and tuition have a set yearly allowance.
• Janus Soft reimburses up to $3,000.00 per year toward skills and tuition.

08  ·  Tools
Health and technology
A yearly allowance covers the tools and programs people actually use.
• Janus Soft reimburses up to $1,000.00 per calendar year for health and technology products and programs, including laptops, gym memberships, cell phones, and tablets.
